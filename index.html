How to Convert Images to PDF (Step-by-Step)

  1. Select your images — click the drop zone or drag and drop any number of PNG, JPG, or WebP files.
  2. Preview thumbnails — a live preview shows you exactly which images are queued.
  3. Click "Convert to PDF" — the tool compiles all images into a single PDF, each on a new page.
  4. Download instantly — your new PDF is saved directly to your device, with no waiting.

Merge PDFs Like a Pro

Whether you're combining scanned receipts, merging chapters of an eBook, or assembling a portfolio, our merge tool lets you combine any number of PDF files in the order you select. The process preserves formatting, fonts, and images exactly as they appear in the source files, thanks to the power of pdf-lib.

Convert Word to PDF Without Losing Quality

Using mammoth, we extract text content from your .docx files and rebuild it as a clean PDF. While complex layouts (tables, headers, footers) are not fully preserved, the core textual content is reliably transferred — perfect for resumes, articles, and plain text documents.

Extract Text from PDF to Word

Our PDF to Word converter uses pdf.js to read the text content from your PDF and create a new .docx file. This is ideal for repurposing content from reports, academic papers, or old documents into editable Word format. While it doesn't preserve complex formatting, it reliably extracts all readable text.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a limit on file size or number of files?

No — everything is processed locally. The only limit is your device's available memory and browser capabilities. You can convert hundreds of images or merge dozens of PDFs without any restrictions.

Do you store my files on your servers?

Absolutely not. All processing happens client-side. Your files never leave your computer. We have no access to your data, and we cannot recover or view any of your documents.

What image formats are supported for Image to PDF?

We support PNG, JPG, JPEG, and WebP. Each image becomes a separate page in the final PDF, preserving the original aspect ratio and quality.

Can I merge password‑protected PDFs?

Currently, the tool does not support encrypted or password‑protected PDFs. You'll need to unlock them first using a PDF unlocker tool.

Is this service really free?

Yes — completely free, with no hidden charges or sign-up requirements. We display non-intrusive ads to keep the service running.

Does the tool work offline?

After the first visit, all libraries are cached by your browser. You can use the tools offline (except for loading ads) as long as you don't clear your cache.

What if I have a large PDF for text extraction?

The PDF to Word converter can handle large PDFs, but performance depends on your device's processing power. For very large files, we recommend splitting the PDF into smaller parts first.

Can I convert Word documents with complex formatting?

Our Word to PDF converter extracts plain text content. Complex formatting like tables, columns, and embedded images are not preserved. For those, we recommend using a desktop word processor.
